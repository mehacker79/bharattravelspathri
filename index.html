Terms & Conditions

Last updated: . By booking with Bharat Travels Pathri you agree to the following.

1. Booking & Advance

A booking is confirmed only after our team confirms the date and the advance payment is received. WhatsApp enquiries alone do not guarantee a bus.

2. Availability

Date availability shown on the website/WhatsApp is indicative; final confirmation is given by Bharat Travels Pathri.

3. Payment & Rent

Rent is agreed per trip/per km/per day at booking. Balance must be paid before or at trip start. Extra distance or extra days are charged additionally.

4. Extra Charges

Toll tax, parking, border/permit tax, driver allowance, and night-halt charges are payable by the customer as per actuals.

5. Cancellation

Cancellation 7+ days before the trip: advance refunded after deducting 10% service charge. Within 7 days: advance may be forfeited in part or full. Cancellation by us for technical/unforeseen reasons: full refund or alternate bus.

6. Passenger Conduct & Safety

  • Seating limited to 42 passengers; no overloading.
  • No smoking, alcohol, gutkha/spitting, or illegal items inside the bus.
  • Damage to seats, glass, music system or interior will be charged to the customer.
  • Driver's safety instructions and legal timings must be followed. Night driving limits apply.

7. Breakdown & Delays

In case of breakdown we will arrange repair or a replacement at the earliest. We are not liable for delays due to traffic, weather, strikes or road conditions.

8. Liability

Passengers are responsible for their luggage and valuables. Liability is limited to the amount of rent paid.

9. Jurisdiction

Disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of Parbhani, Maharashtra courts. Contact: bharattravelspathri@gmail.com.
